About HQFAST Engine

Solving QMA-Hard & NP-Hard Compute Challenges

HQFAST is a physical simulation engine designed to bridge the gap between classical HPC tensor networks and quantum hardware processing units (QPUs).

By executing Matrix Product States (MPS-DMRG) classically down to Chemical Accuracy (≤ 1.6 mHa), HQFAST eliminates unneeded QPU sampling costs while guaranteeing complete hardware independence across superconducting, trapped-ion, and neutral-atom backends.

Our Core Principles

  • Strict Scientific Rigor: Zero hardcoded or mocked verdicts. Every result is dynamically calculated.
  • Configurable Cost Guard: Real-time budget safeguards preventing cloud financial runaway.
  • Domain Neutrality: Pluggable architecture ready for battery chemistry, green catalysis, and combinatorial optimization.
